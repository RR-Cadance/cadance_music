SteadyPulse Placebo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SteadyPulse Placebo iOS app ("SteadyPulse Placebo", "we", "us", or "our") handles information. SteadyPulse Placebo is published by Rubber Rhino. We have intentionally built SteadyPulse Placebo to collect as little information as possible.

In short:
  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • If you start a Heart-Rate Workout, the app reads live heart-rate data and saves the workout to Apple Health. We do not receive, upload, or share that data.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising.

Information we do not collect

SteadyPulse Placebo does not ask you to create an account and does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not collect, upload, sell, share, or use your information for advertising or tracking.

Apple Health data

If you choose to start a Heart-Rate Workout, SteadyPulse Placebo asks for permission to read live heart-rate data and to save the workout you started. This supports compatible paired sensors, including Polar H10 and AirPods Pro 3, and lets the app display a live trend or optionally match the heartbeat sound to your measured rate.

The workout and its heart-rate samples are saved in Apple Health on your device. SteadyPulse Placebo does not operate a server for this data and does not transmit it to Rubber Rhino or other third parties. Apple Health may handle your data according to your Apple Health and iCloud settings; see Apple's Health privacy information.

Purchases

SteadyPulse Placebo is fully unlocked. It offers optional consumable in-app purchase tips to support development. Tips are processed by Apple through the App Store and do not unlock content or features.

Apple processes purchase and transaction details needed to complete a tip. We do not receive your Apple ID, payment card, or billing information. SteadyPulse Placebo does not create accounts, retain purchase entitlements, or use purchase information to track you.

  • Apple — see Apple's Privacy Policy.

On-device settings

Your preferences — such as your selected beats-per-minute, Heart Rate Variability toggle, rate-following setting, and heart-rate zone boundaries — are stored only on your device. They are not transmitted to us.

Children

SteadyPulse is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Network use

SteadyPulse Placebo plays audio without an internet connection. Network access is used only for standard App Store services, including an optional tip purchase if you choose to make one.

Not a medical device

SteadyPulse Placebo is designed for focus, relaxation, pacing, and sound. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, monitor, or treat any condition.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the heartbeat speed?

On the main screen, use the minus and plus buttons or drag along the gauge to set any pace from 40 to 150 BPM. You can change the pace while it is playing; SteadyPulse Placebo moves smoothly to the new rhythm without cutting a beat short.

What does the Heart Rate Variability toggle do?

When turned on, it adds a gentle, natural drift around your chosen pace so the rhythm "breathes" slightly, rather than staying perfectly mechanical. It's optional — turn it off for a perfectly steady beat.

Does the sound keep playing when my screen is off?

Yes. SteadyPulse Placebo continues to play in the background and with the screen locked.

Which headphones or speakers work best?

The heartbeat is a low, soft sound, so it comes through best on anything that seals or fills the ear, or that pushes some air. In-ear and over-ear headphones (wired or Bluetooth) and Bluetooth speakers all work well. We don't recommend open-ear or bone-conduction earphones — the kind that leave your ear canal open or rest on your cheekbone — because they struggle to reproduce the low frequencies and make the heartbeat hard to hear. Your phone's built-in speaker is fine too.

What is free?

Everything is free and fully unlocked: heartbeat playback, Heart Rate Variability, background audio, Heart-Rate Workouts, compatible sensor support, the live display and trend, zones, and optional rate following. There are no subscriptions or feature purchases.

What are the optional tips for?

Tips are an optional way to support development. They unlock nothing: the app remains fully available whether or not you make a tip. Tips are consumable purchases, so there is no restore action.

Can I use SteadyPulse Placebo with haptic wearables?

The steady per-beat audio can work well with compatible body-worn haptic wearables, including subwoofer-style products such as the Woojer Strap 4. SteadyPulse Placebo is not affiliated with Woojer. Woojer and Strap 4 are trademarks of their respective owners.

Can SteadyPulse Placebo follow a Polar H10?

Yes. Pair the H10 in iPhone Settings > Bluetooth, then start a Heart-Rate Workout in SteadyPulse Placebo and authorize Apple Health. Once a live reading appears, you can enable Follow live heart rate. The workout and its heart-rate samples are saved to Apple Health; SteadyPulse Placebo does not upload or share them. For reliable coexistence with Polar apps, turn on the H10's Heart rate visible to all and 2 Bluetooth devices options.

Can I use AirPods Pro 3 for heart rate?

Yes, with directly connected AirPods Pro 3 and iOS 26 or later. Start a Heart-Rate Workout and authorize Apple Health. AirPods heart rate does not work when Woojer Strap 4 is selected as the audio output, so use a Polar H10 for that setup.

What is a Heart-Rate Workout?

It is a workout you explicitly start in SteadyPulse Placebo to collect live heart-rate data from a compatible paired sensor. It shows your live rate, a rolling 60-minute trend, heart-rate zones, and session maximum. You can end it at any time; the completed workout is saved to Apple Health.

Can I choose the audio output?

Yes. Use the Audio Output control on the main screen to open the iPhone system picker. iOS controls the available routes, and SteadyPulse Placebo cannot force or split Bluetooth audio between two devices.

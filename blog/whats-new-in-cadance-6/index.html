What's new in Cadance v6

Cadance v6 release — September 2026

Cadance v6 is about keeping your class plan and your music in the same place. It introduces a free Teacher Notebook, more focused ways to access music, and a richer Artist workspace for teaching material.

Teacher Notebook, free in Class Planning Mode

Teacher Notebook gives every teacher a place to build a plan beside the music. Type a detailed class outline or handwrite an idea, add links to the relevant tracks, and share the note when it is ready. Anyone can read, share, and sample linked music; playback follows the relevant music and access rules.

Move from a note straight into the music

A Cadance link belongs beside the correction, combination, or rehearsal note it supports. Open the track in context and use Focus Player when it is time to stay with the music rather than the controls.

Day Passes and Perfect Practice Passes

Day Passes are for full teaching days. They never expire, and new users receive 14 free Day Passes. Perfect Practice Passes are for one selected song: choose the track you are working on and keep it ready for 30 days of choreography, rehearsal, practice, or filming. New users receive 1 free Perfect Practice Pass.

Focus Player for focused work

Focus Player is available with Artist access and while a Day Pass or Perfect Practice Pass is active. It is the playback space for working through the music without the rest of the app competing for attention.

Artist Syllabus

Syllabus gives Artist subscribers a richer workspace for class material, with support for text, PDFs, images, and video. Teacher Notebook stays free for planning; Syllabus is the place for professional teaching material that needs more depth.

Start planning

Download Cadance on the App Store, create your first Notebook note, and build the next class around the music you choose.

What's new in Cadance 7.5

Cadance 7.5 release — October 2026

Cadance 7 made it faster to find the music you need. Cadance 7.5 builds on that: it gets you to your music sooner, makes importing more dependable, and makes your access easier to understand. Here is everything that has changed since Cadance 6.

One Search for all your music

Tap the magnifier at the top of the screen to search your device library, imported files, Syllabus, playlists, and Apple Music in one place. Your own music comes first, labelled Files, Syllabus, or My Cadance, with Apple Music results below.

Not sure what to type? Start from a grid of dance terms for barre, centre, allegro, pointe work, adagio, waltz, musical feel, and style. Tap two or more to combine them, and add your own terms to the grid. Switch to Your Media to hide Apple Music results, or turn them off in Settings. Without an internet connection, Search still finds your own music.

A simpler start

New installs now show one welcome screen and a single music permission request. Cadance then opens where your music is: your albums or playlists, Cadance Resources, or Apple Music Search. If you choose not to share your music library, Cadance still opens and offers your imported music instead.

Moving from another music service? A new guide shows how to bring your playlists into Cadance, or how to own the tracks you teach with so they play with full tempo control.

Dependable imports

Importing a folder from iCloud Drive now shows how many files are done and can be paused, resumed, or ended, even after you close Cadance. Before it starts, Cadance checks the download size and your free space and warns you before using mobile data.

Files already in Cadance are skipped, so importing again never creates duplicate copies. A Syllabus folder merges with your existing copy and asks before adding missing files, a zip for the Syllabus is kept as it is until you tap to unpack it, and accented names in zips made on a Mac are kept intact.

Apple Music previews

Without an Apple Music subscription, Apple Music tracks now play Apple’s 30-second preview instead of showing an error, with an option to try Apple Music.

Clear guidance when something needs attention

When Albums, Artists, or Syllabus has nothing to show yet, Cadance suggests the next step, such as switching music source, sorting imported items, importing music or Syllabus files, or searching Apple Music.

When a track stops at 15 seconds, Cadance now tells you why and what unlocks it: a subscription or a Day Pass, or for Syllabus tracks, Artist or a pass.

Account & Passes and a new Subscription screen

Settings now gathers your subscription, Day Passes, Perfect Practice Passes, Purchase History, Refresh, and Support Info into one Account & Passes section. Purchase History lists every pass purchase with its date and time.

The new Subscription screen shows your plan, renewal date, and price paid, and lets you manage your subscription with Apple, request a refund, restore purchases, and contact support in one place. Restore Purchases messages are clearer too, including steps to sign in to the App Store when needed.

A new Visual Tour

The built-in Visual Tour was rebuilt for Cadance 7 with a five-tap Quick Start, Passes and Modes, and Troubleshooting. Cadance 7.5 adds a Bluetooth Remote topic and refreshed information panels. Topics you have read are ticked, so you can see what is new.

More dependable in the studio

  • AirPlay: choosing an AirPlay speaker on iPad is more reliable, including with a note docked beside your music.
  • Volume: the volume control shows an exact level only when Cadance can read it reliably, and offers simple − and + buttons otherwise.
  • Apple Watch: tracks started from your Watch now respect No Repeat, and switching between No Repeat and Play All on iPhone takes effect without restarting the track.
  • Your access: your subscription is recognised as soon as iCloud sign-in completes after a reinstall, and reinstalling on the same device keeps today’s active Day Pass.
  • iPhone Duo: Cadance adapts its layout to both the outer and inner displays.

Class Planning mode: How to use Cadance for free

Cadance 4 release — April 2026 · Updated for Cadance 7.5, October 2026

Class Planning mode is the always-free way to use Cadance. It is for planning, not a playback tier: you can browse, search, create playlists, write notes, and preview music without spending anything. Here's how to use it.

What you can do in Class Planning mode

Unlimited, no Day Pass required:

  • Browse your Apple Music library, imported files, and Syllabus
  • Browse all 70 curated Cadance Resources playlists
  • Search your music and Apple Music together, and use "More Like This" discovery
  • Create, edit, rename, re-order, and import playlists
  • Import Apple Music playlists as editable My Cadance playlists
  • Build playlist collages, organize folders, manage Favorites
  • Write, sync, and share typed and handwritten notes in Notebook
  • Import music and Syllabus folders, and read Syllabus documents
  • Preview any track for 15 seconds (including Apple Music catalog tracks)

What requires a Day Pass or subscription

Full-length playback, and the tools you use while teaching:

  • Playing tracks past 15 seconds
  • Countdown to start
  • Apple Watch control
  • Focus Player and notes docked beside the music (Artist or an active Day Pass)

When a track stops at 15 seconds, Cadance tells you what unlocks it. If you have Day Passes, one activates for the day unless you choose not to use it.

The recommended Class Planning workflow

Here's how many teachers use Class Planning mode:

  1. Browse and discover: Search Apple Music or browse the curated Cadance Resources playlists.
  2. Preview: Listen to 15-second previews of tracks to evaluate them for your class.
  3. Create a custom playlist: Build a playlist for your class, adding tracks from any playable source — your local library files, file system files, cloud library files, and anything in the Apple Music library.
  4. Write a class note: In Notebook, type or handwrite your class plan and add links to the tracks right beside the exercise, combination, or correction each one supports. Your notes and your music then stay together in one place.
  5. Activate a Day Pass: When you're ready to teach, activate a Day Pass and play your class playlist, or open your note, dock it beside the music, and tap each track link as you reach it.

Why this matters

Teachers can fully evaluate Cadance's tempo control and interface before paying anything. Studio admins and class planners who don't play music in class may never need to activate a Day Pass. Class prep at home or on the bus is genuinely free — you only pay on the days you actually teach.

For studio admins and planners

If you're a studio admin who plans classes but doesn't play music yourself, Class Planning mode is perfect for you. Create playlists, organize them by class, write class notes with the tracks linked in place, and share them with your instructors. No Day Pass needed.

Get started

Download Cadance on the App Store and start exploring Class Planning mode. You'll also receive 14 free Day Passes and 1 free Perfect Practice Pass to try full playback whenever you're ready.

Your class plan, beside your music

Notebook — August 20, 2026 · Updated for Cadance 7.5, October 2026

Teaching notes tend to live wherever there is room: on paper, in a document, or in a message to yourself. Notebook brings them closer to the music that makes them useful.

Use the format that fits

Write a typed plan when you need detail, or handwrite a quick idea when movement is still taking shape. Notebook is free in Class Planning Mode, on both iPhone and iPad, and your notes sync between them through iCloud.

Link the music to the instruction

Add a Cadance track link beside the phrase, exercise, or routine it supports. When you return to the note, the music is already connected to the reason you chose it.

Share the plan, preserve the context

Notes and their music links can be read, shared, and sampled by everyone. Full-length playback follows the access that covers each track.

Keep the room moving

When it is time to work, dock the note beside your music and open the linked track in Focus Player. Docked notes and Focus Player need Artist, an active Day Pass, or a Perfect Practice Pass.

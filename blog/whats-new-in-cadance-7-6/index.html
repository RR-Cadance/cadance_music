What's new in Cadance 7.6

Cadance 7.6 release — October 2026

Cadance 7.6 gets you to your music faster when you first open it, lets you choose where every class starts, and makes Apple Watch playback controls behave as you'd expect. It builds on Cadance 7.5.

Straight to your music

After the welcome screen, Cadance now opens directly where your music is, such as your albums, your Syllabus, or Cadance Resources, instead of switching screens once it has started. Once you have Favorites, Cadance opens on Favorites.

Welcome messages now appear one at a time. They no longer cover each other or a permission request, and a quick tap can't dismiss the next one by accident. Returning customers see “Welcome Back” with their Day Pass balance, and new customers' welcome passes arrive within seconds.

Choose where Cadance opens

The new Settings › Open Cadance On option lets you pick Automatic, Favorites, Syllabus, Library, or Playlists. Choose Syllabus to start every class on your Syllabus. Your choice takes effect the next time Cadance opens; Automatic keeps the behaviour described above.

Find your imported music

When Cadance can't see your Music library, because access to Media & Apple Music is turned off, it offers to show the music you've imported into Cadance instead. The same offer appears straight after an import finishes, so the files you just added are one tap away.

Apple Music offer

New Apple Accounts that can subscribe to Apple Music now reliably see Apple's offer on first launch.

Apple Watch Pause and Rewind

  • Pause: pausing a track from your Watch no longer makes it start over. In the first 15 seconds, Pause returns to the start (or loop marker A) and stays paused; after that, Pause leaves the track where it is.
  • Rewind: Rewind keeps the track playing or paused, just as it was.
  • Loop marker A: Apple Music tracks paused in the first 15 seconds now return to marker A, like your own music files.

This release also includes stability improvements.

Get Cadance 7.6

Update or download Cadance on the App Store. New users receive 14 free Day Passes and 1 free Perfect Practice Pass. See every feature on the Features page.

One song, thirty days: Perfect Practice Passes

Cadance v5 — June 2, 2026

Some work does not need a whole day of Cadance. It needs one piece of music, returned to again and again until the movement lands. Perfect Practice Passes were made for that work.

Choose the song you are working on

A Perfect Practice Pass keeps one selected track ready for 30 days. It is a focused way to work through a routine, refine choreography, rehearse a section, prepare for competition, or film a take.

Focus on the work, not the player

While a Perfect Practice Pass is active, Focus Player is available for that focused practice session. Pair it with a Notebook note so the instruction, correction, and music remain connected.

Different from a Day Pass

A Day Pass unlocks a full day of Cadance when you are teaching a class. A Perfect Practice Pass stays with one song over time. Choose the one that matches the work in front of you.

Start with a free pass

Every new Cadance user receives 1 free Perfect Practice Pass, alongside 14 free Day Passes. Download Cadance, choose a track, and give the routine the time it needs.

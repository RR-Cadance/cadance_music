Meet Ran Bagno

Featured Artist — July 11, 2026

Ran Bagno is a contemporary dance, theatre, and film composer living and working in Israel. His work brings atmosphere, movement, and narrative clarity to choreography and performance.

Music shaped by movement

Ran's long association with Vertigo Dance Company and work across dance, stage, and screen make his catalog a natural fit for teachers and choreographers looking for music with space, pulse, and intent.

Find Ran Bagno in Cadance

Cadance Resources includes the Ran Bagno Sampler, ready to explore in the app. Add a track to a Notebook note to keep the music close to the phrase or routine it serves.

Privacy Policy

Updated 30 Aug 2026

Summary

We use Microsoft Clarity for user behavior insights to improve the site experience. We don't track you across the web for advertising purposes. The Cadance marketing site is largely static and self‑contained; one optional request form submits the details you choose to share.

What we collect

  • Basic server logs from GitHub Pages (handled by GitHub).
  • User behavior data via Microsoft Clarity for site optimization.
  • Emails you send us at info@cadance.music.
  • Details you submit on our optional art-request form: your name, email address, and—if you choose to provide it—your phone number, along with your SMS consent choice.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising trackers or cross‑site tracking for marketing purposes.
  • No sale of personal data. Ever.

Art-request form

When you request the dance-art note files, we use the name and email you provide solely to send you those files, and—if you opt in with your phone number—to send the remaining notes by text message when that option is available. This information is stored on Cloudflare (which also provides the form's spam protection) and is kept only as long as needed to fulfil your request, then deleted. We never use it for marketing lists or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Email

If you email us, we'll use your message solely to respond and to provide support. We don't add you to mailing lists.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our site. This helps us improve the user experience. Clarity collects anonymous usage data including page views, clicks, and scrolling behavior. This data is not used for advertising purposes. You can learn more about Clarity's privacy practices at Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Third‑party services

Our site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Any limited logging performed by GitHub is subject to their policies. We use Microsoft Clarity as described above. The optional art-request form is processed by Cloudflare (request storage, email delivery, and Turnstile spam protection), which handles the details you submit on our behalf as described in this policy.

Children

Cadance is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we update this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions? Email info@cadance.music.

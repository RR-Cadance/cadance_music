MyCareRoster

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MyCareRoster iOS app ("MyCareRoster", "we", "us", or "our") handles information. MyCareRoster is published by Rubber Rhino. It is a private directory of the health care providers that you and your family see, and it is built so that your information stays with you and your own Apple Account.

In short:
  • We do not collect, receive, or sell your information. We have no server that stores it.
  • There are no accounts to create, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking or SDKs.
  • Your roster syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud account, encrypted on your device first.
  • Private family profiles stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync, and are encrypted on your device first.

Contacts

With your permission, MyCareRoster reads the contacts in your Contacts list named "Medical" to show your providers' names, organizations, phone numbers, email addresses, and addresses. These details are read on your device each time and are not uploaded by MyCareRoster.

MyCareRoster changes Contacts only when you ask it to: creating the Medical list, creating a provider contact, adding contacts you pick to the Medical list, or opening a contact in Apple's contact editor. It never deletes contacts and never writes MyCareRoster's own information into Contacts.

Your roster

Information you add in MyCareRoster — family member names, care teams, provider roles and specialties, primary GP choices, and notes — is stored on your device with iOS Data Protection. To keep your iPhone and iPad in sync, it is also stored in your private iCloud database. Before it leaves your device, it is encrypted with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain, so iCloud holds only encrypted data. Provider names and organizations are included (encrypted) so your devices can match providers; phone numbers, email addresses, and postal addresses are not.

Private family profiles

A private profile can hold a family member's legal name, allergies, medicines, conditions, procedures, and health, insurance, and patient identifiers. Opening it requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS; MyCareRoster never receives biometric data.

Private profiles are encrypted and stored only on your device by default. If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, profiles are encrypted on your device before being stored in your private iCloud database, and only that encrypted data reaches iCloud. You can turn sync off at any time and choose to delete the iCloud copy.

When you copy a value from a private profile, it is placed on this device's clipboard only (not shared with your other devices) and expires after two minutes.

iCloud

MyCareRoster uses Apple's CloudKit private database, which belongs to your Apple Account. We cannot access your private iCloud data, and the encryption described above means its contents are not readable by Apple either. How Apple handles iCloud is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

MyCareRoster has a free version with limits on the number of family members, care teams, and providers. The optional MyCareRoster Unlimited is a one-time in-app purchase, processed by Apple, that removes those limits. We do not receive your Apple ID, payment card, or billing information. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device.

Sharing

MyCareRoster shares information only when you choose to, for example by calling or emailing a provider, which opens Apple's Phone or Mail app. We do not share your information with anyone.

Support information

If you contact us for help, we may ask you to open MyCareRoster's Support Info screen and copy its contents into your message. The app never sends it automatically; we receive it only if you paste it into a message to us. It contains the app version and build, how the app was installed, your device model, iOS version, language, region, and time zone; Contacts permission, Medical list status, and the number of contacts in the list; iCloud and sync status, including whether sync is on, when your roster and private profiles were last saved, whether this device has its encryption key, and the most recent iCloud error (an error description only, never your data); counts of family members, teams, providers, unavailable providers, pending reconnections, private profiles, and profile versions to review; your MyCareRoster Unlimited purchase status and purchase environment; whether the sample family is shown; whether Face ID or Touch ID is available; and two identifiers: your MyCareRoster Unlimited transaction ID and your iCloud user record ID for MyCareRoster (a random identifier that doesn't reveal your Apple ID). It never includes names, contact details, notes, or any private profile or health information. We use it only to answer your request.

Deleting your information

Deleting MyCareRoster from a device removes the information stored on that device. To remove the encrypted copies in iCloud, turn off private profile sync and choose to delete the iCloud copy, and remove MyCareRoster's data from iCloud in the Settings app under your Apple Account > iCloud. MyCareRoster's Diagnostics section, shown by tapping the version number in its Settings seven times, can also delete its iCloud copies, or delete everything it has stored, including the iCloud copies and its encryption keys, after Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Your contacts remain in Contacts.

Children

MyCareRoster lets a parent or carer record information about family members, including children. That information stays on the user's devices and private iCloud account as described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

MyCareRoster organizes contact details and information you enter. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, monitor, or treat any condition or give medical advice.

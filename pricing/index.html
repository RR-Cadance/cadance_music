Pricing & plans

Choose access that matches the work in front of you. New users receive 14 free Day Passes and 1 free Perfect Practice Pass.

Choose your access

Class Planning Mode is always free: browse, search, create playlists, write and share notes in Notebook, and hear 15-second previews. No pass is required to plan.

Day Passes

For teaching days

Full Artist-level access for one calendar day, activated when a track plays past 15 seconds. Day Passes never expire.

Perfect Practice Pass

For one chosen song

Keep one track ready for 30 days of choreography, rehearsal, practice, or filming, with two free swaps.

The Spark

Your music, full class tools

The BarreNotes-equivalent playback tier for regular teaching.

  • Full playback of music stored on your device and imported files
  • Countdown to start
  • Apple Watch control for everything Spark plays

The Artist

Most Popular

Apple Music and the full teaching workspace

For teachers who use Apple Music and richer professional class material.

  • Everything in Spark
  • Apple Music tracks, streamed or downloaded, including Cadance Resources playlists
  • Focus Player and notes docked beside the music
  • Syllabus playback, with text, PDFs, images, and video

How access works

Cadance is free to download. Class Planning Mode includes browsing, search, playlists, Notebook, sharing, and 15-second previews. A pass or subscription unlocks full-length playback. When a track stops at 15 seconds, Cadance tells you what unlocks it.

Bring your own music

Cadance isn’t bundled with music and doesn’t sell music. Import and organize your files at any time; Spark and above play music stored on your device and imported files in full. Apple Music tracks need Artist or a Day Pass, and your own Apple Music subscription. Without one, Apple Music tracks play Apple’s 30-second preview.

Manage your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and renewed by Apple. In Cadance, Settings › Account & Passes › Subscription shows your plan and renewal date, and lets you manage your subscription with Apple, request a refund, or restore purchases.

Business expense note

For many teachers, a Cadance subscription may be a deductible business expense depending on your country and circumstances.

Pricing questions

How does Cadance pricing work?

Cadance combines free Class Planning Mode, flexible passes, and optional subscriptions. New users receive 14 free Day Passes and 1 free Perfect Practice Pass. A Day Pass unlocks a full teaching day, while a Perfect Practice Pass keeps one selected song ready for 30 days. Day Passes never expire.

What can I do with Cadance for free?

Class Planning Mode includes browsing, search, playlist creation and editing, Notebook, sharing, discovery, and 15-second previews of any track. Full-length playback needs a pass or subscription that covers the track.

Do Day Passes expire?

No. Day Passes never expire. They stay in your account until you activate them, ready whenever you teach.

Will pricing be different in my country?

Often, yes. Apple sets local prices and currencies. The App Store always shows the final price before you subscribe.

Which subscription tier is right for me?

Choose Day Passes for flexible full teaching days, or a Perfect Practice Pass when one song is the focus. Spark covers music stored on your device and imported files, with Apple Watch control; Artist adds Apple Music, Focus Player, and Syllabus playback. Current local pricing appears in the App Store.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Only for Apple Music tracks. Music you own and files you import never need one. To play Apple Music tracks in full, you need your own Apple Music subscription plus Artist or a Day Pass. Without Apple Music, those tracks play Apple’s 30-second preview.

How do I change, cancel, or get a refund for my subscription?

Open Settings › Account & Passes › Subscription in Cadance. Manage Subscription opens Apple’s subscription settings, and Request a Refund opens Apple’s refund request for your current subscription. For a pass purchase, Purchase History links to Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Can I subscribe AND buy Day Passes?

Yes. A Day Pass temporarily unlocks Artist-tier access regardless of your current subscription. For example, a Spark subscriber can buy a Day Pass to access Apple Music for a one-off workshop without upgrading their subscription.

What if I run out of Day Passes mid-class?

That won't happen. Once a Day Pass activates, you have full access until midnight. Cadance will never interrupt a class. The only check happens at the 15-second mark of the first track of the day that needs a pass — well before any class begins.
